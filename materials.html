Detailed Materials

PLA

Best for Prototypes

Easy-to-print, eco-friendly material ideal for visual models and basic prototypes.

Best For
  • Concept models
  • Display parts
  • Low-stress applications
Not Recommended For
  • Heat exposure
  • Mechanical load

ABS

Strong & Durable

Tough engineering plastic with good heat resistance.

Best For
  • Functional parts
  • Enclosures
  • Automotive components

PETG

Balanced Material

Combines the strength of ABS and the ease of printing of PLA.

Best For
  • Mechanical parts
  • Transparent components
  • Outdoor use

TPU

Flexible Material

Rubber-like material for flexible applications and impact resistance.

Best For
  • Gaskets & Seals
  • Wearables
  • Shock absorption parts

Nylon

Engineering Grade

High strength and wear-resistant material for demanding applications.

Best For
  • Gears & Bearings
  • Functional prototypes
  • Load-bearing parts

Resin

High Detail

Used in SLA printing for ultra-smooth and incredibly detailed parts.

Best For
  • Miniatures
  • Jewelry casting
  • Dental models
